Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. About This Application

Social Media Automation is a personal application designed to automate video publishing to authorized social media accounts.

2. Google Account Access

The application requests permission to upload videos to the user's authorized YouTube channel and to access selected Google Drive files.

3. Data Usage

Google Drive files are accessed for video publishing. Google account data is used only to provide the application's requested functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored in private GitHub Actions secrets. Videos and their descriptions remain in the user's Google Drive account.

5. Data Sharing

Personal Google account information is not sold to advertisers or data brokers. Authorized videos are transferred to the selected publishing platform.

6. Revoking Access

Users can revoke Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
